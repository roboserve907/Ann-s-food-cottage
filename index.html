Meet the founder

Founder, CEO & Chef — Annie

I’m Annie, a Nigerian-born chef, entrepreneur, and mental health advocate based in Accra, Ghana. Ann’s Food Cottage grew from my love for food, creativity, and bringing people together through memorable dining experiences.

Food is comfort, connection, celebration, and an expression of love. I’m involved in every part of the journey, creating dishes and building a brand rooted in quality, warmth, authenticity, and excellent service.

Beyond the kitchen, I use my voice to encourage open conversations about mental health and remind people that it is okay to ask for help and prioritise well-being. Through Ann’s Food Cottage, I hope to serve delicious food, create meaningful experiences, and build something rooted in passion, purpose, and people.
